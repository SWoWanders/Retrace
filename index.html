RETRACE

Turn a walking video into a simple 3D street you can fly through along the same path. Everything runs on this device. No uploads, no accounts, no cost.

  • Walk forward smoothly. Avoid spinning on the spot or pointing into the sun.
  • FOV: ~70° for a normal phone camera, ~100–120° for ultra-wide or action cams.
  • People and vehicles are cut out of the blocks and shown as a live 3D layer instead (small extra model, ~4–15 MB).
  • Edited videos: each shot becomes its own piece, placed side by side. Still-camera shots become a diorama: a frozen 3D set with the moving parts playing inside it.
  • Anything else works too (dancing, animation, screen recordings…). Shots where the camera can't be tracked become a 3D video stage, where every frame plays as a moving 3D surface.
  • The first run downloads a free depth model (~27–100 MB), which is cached after that.
